# Executive Summary

TFC Optical (300394.SZ) shows strong Q3 2026 momentum from AI-driven optical device demand. Upstream material constraints eased, enabling sequential revenue growth; strategic inventory builds and CPO product ramp position it for 2027 hyperscaler volume.

# Key Findings

- Q3 revenue RMB 1.923B (+31.5% YoY, +28.4% QoQ); 9M revenue RMB 4.751B (+21.3% YoY), net profit RMB 2.083B (+42.2% YoY).
- Active/passive output rose; core materials supply improved, supporting delivery.
- Inventory up 179% YoY to RMB 1.274B on strategic prep for CPO/NPO and customer demand.
- CPO FAU/ELS products in volume delivery; 2027 share expected to rise materially. NPO still in sampling/validation.
- Hyperscaler optical demand robust: 800G/1.6T acceleration, laser capacity tight into 2029, Chinese suppliers dominant in modules.

# Evidence and Sources

- Company Q3 2026 report and analyst notes (Citic, Changjiang, Oct-Nov 2026).
- Industry: Lumentum laser sold out to early 2029; AAOI 1.6T orders about USD 450M; Cignal AI scale-across optical CAGR 27%; Counterpoint Chinese module share about 66%; Cisco hyperscaler AI revenue target USD 9B FY27.

# Analytical Reasoning

Material relief unlocked active-device volume, driving sequential acceleration while passive demand stayed elevated. Inventory and equipment prepayments signal confidence in sustained AI interconnect orders. CPO positioning captures the next wave of co-packaged optics as hyperscalers move beyond pluggables. Thailand capacity expansion supports overseas diversification.

# Risks and Limitations

- Potential US restrictions on Chinese optics could disrupt hyperscaler supply chains.
- Upstream laser/InP tightness may re-emerge.
- CPO ramp timing depends on customer validation and foundry yields.
- Limited public order-book granularity.

# Independent Conclusion

Supply-chain signals are constructive for TFC: improved input availability, proactive stocking, and early CPO traction align with hyperscaler and telecom optical expansion into 2027.
